METHOD: STATIC VAT REVENUE FROM ABOLISHING TAX-FREE REFUNDS (vat_return_abolition_data.py)
=======================================================================================

This note describes how vat_return_abolition_data.py estimates the VAT revenue the EU-27
would gain if VAT refunds to non-EU tourists (tax-free shopping) were abolished. The
estimate is static: it assumes no behavioural response, i.e. tourists are assumed to buy
the same goods even without a refund.

All values are in EUR. "bn" = billion, "m" = million.


1. OUTPUT
---------

Static VAT revenue bound: EUR 2.4-4.0 bn per year (central EUR 3.0 bn).

    Step                                Low      Central   High
    1 Tax-free sales in store (EUR bn)  24.1     24.1      24.1
    2 x Continental Europe share        72%      72.4%     78%
    3 x EU-27 share                     74%      85%       90%
    4 / Global Blue market share        75%      70%       60%
    = S (EUR bn)                        17.1     21.2      28.2
    5 x VAT content                     17.2%    17.2%     17.2%
    6 x Refund ratio                    0.83     0.83      0.83
    = R_static (EUR bn)                 2.4      3.0       4.0

The low and high cases combine the three uncertain parameters (steps 2-4) at their 
extremes in the same direction.


2. FORMULA AND SCOPE
--------------------

    R_static = S x tau / (1 + tau) x rho

    S     value of tax-free sales in the EU-27 for which an export form is issued,
          including VAT
    tau   weighted average standard VAT rate on those sales
    rho   share of issued forms that are refunded

    S = Global Blue tax-free sales in store x Continental Europe share x EU-27 share
        / Global Blue market share

Period: Global Blue's financial year 2024/25 (April 2024 to March 2025).


3. STEPS
--------

Step 1: Global Blue tax-free sales in store, FY2024/25: EUR 24.1 bn

- Nine months to December 2024: tax-free sales in store EUR 18.3 bn, group sales in
  store EUR 25.0 bn [1]. Tax-free sales in store are the value, including VAT, of goods
  bought by international shoppers.
- Full year FY2024/25: group sales in store EUR 32.9 bn [2, 3].
- [ASSUMPTION] The filings stop at December, so Q4 (January-March 2025) is
  extrapolated. Q4 group sales in store are EUR 32.9 bn - 25.0 bn = EUR 7.9 bn. Applying
  the nine-month tax-free share of 73.2% gives EUR 5.8 bn, so the full year is EUR 24.1
  bn.
- Cross-checks: extrapolating with Global Blue's take rate (tax-free revenue / tax-free
  sales) gives EUR 24.2 bn [1, 2]; 41 m transactions at an average of EUR 587 give EUR
  24.1 bn [4].

Step 2: Continental Europe share of sales: 72.4% (range 72-78%)

- Tax-free revenue FY2024/25: Continental Europe EUR 321.3 m, Asia Pacific EUR 63.2 m
  [2].
- Revenue is converted to sales using the VAT content of each region. Asia-Pacific
  destination weights (Japan 65%, Singapore 27%, South Korea 8% of 2023 tax-free spend
  [5]) and statutory rates (Japan 10%, South Korea 10%, Singapore 9%) give a VAT content
  of 8.9%, against 17.2% for Europe (step 5).
- [ASSUMPTION] Global Blue's revenue per euro of sales is proportional to the VAT
  content of the sale. This leaves out differences in merchant mix and average spend.
- Central: (321.3 / 0.172) / (321.3 / 0.172 + 63.2 / 0.089) = 72.4%.
- [ASSUMPTION] Low case 70%. High case 78%.

Step 3: EU-27 share of Continental Europe: 85% (range 74-90%)

- Switzerland is part of Global Blue's Continental Europe segment [6].
- France, Italy, Spain and Germany together make up 74% of Continental European spend
  [5]. Non-EU destinations fall within the remaining 26%, so the EU-27 share is at least
  74% (low case).
- [ASSUMPTION] Central 85% and high 90%.

Step 4: Gross-up from Global Blue to the whole market: / 0.70 (range 0.60-0.75)

- Global Blue reports a market share of about 70% in tax-free shopping, based on sales
  in store, more than three times larger than the second-largest provider (about 20%)
  [7].
- [ASSUMPTION] The worldwide market share applies to the EU-27. The low market share
  of 60% (high case) allows for the competitor Planet being stronger in Europe than
  worldwide. The high market share of 75% (low case) allows for the self-reported figure
  being conservative.
- Retailers that refund customers themselves are not covered, so S is understated by
  an amount that could not be sized.

Step 5: VAT content: tau / (1 + tau) = 17.2%

- 2023 destination shares of tax-free spend: France 26%, Italy 25%, Spain 15%, Germany
  8% [5]. Their standard VAT rates are 20%, 22%, 21% and 19%. Weighted, tau = 20.8%, so
  tau / (1 + tau) = 17.2%.
- [ASSUMPTION] The rest of the EU has the same average VAT rate as the four largest
  destinations.
- [ASSUMPTION] Every sale bears the standard rate. French customs data imply a VAT
  content of 15.9% instead of 16.7% at the standard rate, so this step may overstate
  VAT by about 5%.

Step 6: Refund ratio: rho = 0.83

- Global Blue's refund ratio, by value of sales in store, was 83% as of 31 March 2024
  [8].
- [ASSUMPTION] Global Blue's refund ratio applies to the whole market and still holds
  in FY2024/25.


4. KNOWN BIASES
---------------

Two known biases push the true value above the central EUR 3.0 bn:
- in-house refunds by retailers are not covered (step 4);

One bias pushes it slightly below: the standard-rate assumption (step 5).
